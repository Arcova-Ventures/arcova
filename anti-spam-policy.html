Anti-Spam Policy

Arcova does not send, authorize, or condone unsolicited bulk email. This policy applies to all employees, contractors, agents, and any third-party service sending on Arcova's behalf.

We do not purchase, rent, or use scraped email lists for bulk messaging.

We do not operate an affiliate program, and no third party is authorized to promote Arcova, arcova.vc, or any Arcova property through unsolicited messages.

Email from arcova.vc is limited to direct correspondence with individuals who have an existing business relationship with us, who have contacted us, or who have otherwise consented to be contacted.

Any outbound business development we conduct will honor opt-out requests promptly, will identify the sender clearly, and will comply with applicable law including the CAN-SPAM Act.

Violations of this policy by any employee, contractor, or vendor will result in termination of the relevant access or engagement.

To report a violation, contact brian@arcova.vc.
